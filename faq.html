Sign Up / Login

Click "Sign Up" in the top navigation or "Start Planning Free" on the homepage. You'll be asked to provide your email and create a password. It's completely free to create an account.
On the login page, click "Forgot Password" and enter your email address. We'll send you a link to reset your password.
You can browse vendors without an account, but you'll need to create a free account to save your event plan, message vendors, and manage bookings.
Currently, we support email-based sign up. Social media login options are coming soon!

Privacy & Data

We use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your data. Your personal information is never sold to third parties. For more details, see our Privacy Policy.
We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email, event details, and preferences. We also collect usage data to improve our service. You can view and update your information in your account settings.
Yes, you can delete your account at any time through your account settings. This will permanently remove your personal data from our systems.
Your event details are private and only shared with vendors you choose to contact. Vendors can only see the information you provide when messaging them.

Billing & Payments

Yes! Creating an account and using our planning tools is completely free. You only pay vendors directly for their services. We don't charge any fees for using the platform.
Payments are made directly to vendors according to their payment terms. CleverPlanners facilitates the connection but doesn't process payments. You'll work out payment details directly with each vendor.
Payment methods vary by vendor. Most vendors accept credit cards, bank transfers, and checks. Discuss payment options directly with your chosen vendors.
No hidden fees. CleverPlanners is free to use. You only pay vendors for their services at the prices they quote you.

Yes, vendor profiles include reviews and ratings from other users. You can view these when browsing vendor details.
If you encounter any issues, please contact us through our contact page. We'll do our best to help resolve the situation.
Vendors can sign up through our vendor onboarding page. We'll review your application and get you set up on the platform.
