About

Grand Ballroom Events is a premier event venue located in the heart of New York City. With over 10 years of experience, we specialize in hosting elegant weddings, bar mitzvahs, and corporate events. Our stunning ballroom features high ceilings, crystal chandeliers, and state-of-the-art sound and lighting systems. We offer full-service event planning, catering coordination, and flexible space configurations to accommodate events from 50 to 500 guests.
